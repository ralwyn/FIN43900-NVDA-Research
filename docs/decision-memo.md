## Decision: watch / defer initiation
Keep NVIDIA on the watch list. The dated DCF supports a rounded scenario span of $100-$285 per diluted share, with a $158 base, against the September 24 close of $224.58. The upside case can support initiation, but the base does not provide a margin of safety. These are causal scenarios, not probability-weighted price targets. Request permission to continue research, with no position initiated on this evidence alone.

## What supports the operating forecast
The latest included reported balance sheet is July 26, 2026, published August 26. The first-half revenue input and Q3 revenue guidance support near-term scale; Q4 revenue of $118.8bn is a separate judgment, not guidance. Full FY2027 revenue is $404.6bn. Later revenue growth fades to 35%, 25%, 15% and 8%; full-year gross margin fades from 74% to 70%, while R&D rises from 8% to 12% of revenue. The model personalizes working capital, R&D and supplier inventory for a fabless semiconductor business.

## Valuation and normalization
FCFF comes from the operating statements, with WACC derived from a 12% required equity return, 4.5% borrowing cost, 17% tax rate and market-equity/debt weights. These financing rates are judgment inputs tested over a range, not a fitted beta estimate. Terminal reinvestment funds stable productive assets and working capital at 3% growth. The FY2026 H20 charge is separately normalized for profitability context; it is not added again to future cash flow. Forecast earnings exclude investment gains; SBC stays a compensation cost without a free cash-flow addback.

The equity bridge adds estimated excess cash, marketable securities and haircut private investments once, and subtracts $33.366bn reported debt. It uses 24.285bn diluted weighted-average shares as a fixed proxy; 24.1bn basic shares are used only for market-cap weights. Preferred equity and NCI are assumed absent. Financial investments and debt are not silently updated to October prices.

## What could change the decision
Under the base operating forecast, the market price requires approximately 9.32% WACC versus the base 11.95%. A growth-only shift within the tested +/-10 percentage-point domain does not reach the quote. This is a conditional market-implied expectation, not proof the market is wrong.

Use a provisional initiation gate of price at or below 80% of an independently defended base value (about $126 on this model), or a revised base above about $281 at the $224.58 reference price, subject to acceptable downside and refreshed evidence. Reversal triggers include failed customer returns on AI infrastructure, export-policy shocks, persistent margin erosion, weaker collections, or increasing supplier commitments without cash conversion. A downside outcome moves the name to do-not-initiate until the economics recover. Thresholds are decision rules for review, not instructions to trade.

## Cross-method disagreement
At the same September 24 closing-price date, qualified AMD/Broadcom annual GAAP P/E references imply roughly $360-$1,164 for NVIDIA. EV/sales references imply $242-$266. Neither is an adopted fair-value interval. Their annual financial and share-count dates differ; AMD has CPU/client/gaming exposure and relatively small historical earnings, while Broadcom combines semiconductors with software and tax-sensitive earnings. Revenue multiples omit differences in margin and reinvestment. Do not average these references with the DCF. Their disagreement points to growth-duration and earnings-definition risk. No control transaction multiple is admitted: VMware's software/control/synergy and transaction-date context is not transferable to NVIDIA minority shares.

## Sensitivity and limits
With operating assumptions fixed, +/-5-point growth shifts produce about $133-$186/share; +/-2-point margin shifts produce $152-$164. Growth ranks higher only over those ranges. Reducing its range to +/-1 point reverses the ranking against the same margin range. Terminal value supplies 74% of base operating EV, making the discount-rate and steady-state assumptions material.

September balances are estimated by a straight-line roll from July's reported balance sheet, and remaining FY2027 cash flows are prorated on the same basis. This fixes the earlier whole-year discounting mismatch but is not a daily forecast. Nonoperating investment values, no additional acquisitions/investments/buybacks, fixed shares, aggregate operating accruals, taxes and leases remain simplifications. Revisit the work on the next filing, a guidance/export-control change, or a price crossing the stated gates. This dated analysis does not assert an October 19 market value.
